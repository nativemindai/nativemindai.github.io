## The short version
Since Nov 17, 2026, every artist on Spotify can turn on Artist Profile Protection: Spotify emails you when music is delivered with your name on it.
AI fakes are piling up (Sony Music alone has asked platforms to take down more than 260,000), and this is how you keep them off your profile.

## Do this
1. Log in to Spotify for Artists at **artists.spotify.com**, or open the Spotify for Artists app. You need to be an **Admin** or **Editor** on your artist team.
2. Open **Artist Profile Protection**. On a computer, click your profile picture to open the menu (on some versions it's under **Settings**). In the app, tap **Music**, then the **Review** tab.
3. Turn it on and keep the default setting, **Review releases**. Don't see it yet? It may still be reaching your account.
4. Watch your inbox. When the email arrives, open **Music > Review** and remove anything that isn't yours: select **Remove** next to it.

## Watch out
- **Ignore the email and it goes live.** With Review releases, a release goes live as scheduled with your name on it unless you remove it. You have up to 28 days after it goes live to act.
- **Removing takes your name off. It doesn't take the song down.** A removed release won't show on your profile, count toward your stats or be recommended to your listeners, but it stays on Spotify. To report it, use Spotify's help center.
- **Spotify only.** It flags every release with your name on it, AI or not, but only on Spotify. A release you remove can still show under your name on other services. For those, go through your distributor.
- **Some releases skip the check.** A release that reuses tracks already on your profile can go live without asking you, and releases from some providers can't be removed this way.
- **Block mode is stricter.** **Block releases**, under **Advanced settings**, holds every release until you approve it. Approvals can take up to 24 hours, and a release you don't approve won't list you and may not go live on time. If you put out a lot of collabs, each one waits on you.
- **Keep your artist key secret.** Block mode gives you an artist key that lets trusted distributors send pre-approved releases. Share it only with distributors you trust, never post it, and if it leaks, contact Spotify to refresh it.
