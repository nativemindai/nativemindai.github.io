## The short version
Black Friday is Friday, Nov 27, Cyber Monday is Nov 30, and lots of plugin deals are already live.
Before you spend anything, have AI check what you already own, so you only pay to fill a real gap.

## Do this
1. Screenshot your plugin list. In FL Studio, click **Options → Manage plugins**. The list shows each plugin with its type and vendor. In Ableton Live, click **Plug-Ins** under **Library** in the browser, then open each folder. Take as many screenshots as you need, and crop out any folder path that shows your name.
2. Open Claude or ChatGPT, add your screenshots with the **+** button, then paste this prompt and fill in the brackets:

   ```prompt
   I attached screenshots of the plugins I own. Audit them before I spend money on Black Friday deals.

   My DAW and edition: [MY DAW]
   My genre: [MY GENRE]
   My budget: [MY BUDGET]

   1. List every plugin you can read in the screenshots. Mark any name you can't read clearly.
   2. Group the plugins that do the same job (EQ, compressor, reverb, delay, saturation, synth, sampler and so on). For each group, say which one to keep using, in one line.
   3. List the jobs the stock plugins in my DAW already cover, so I don't pay for them twice.
   4. Name the ONE gap worth filling for my genre within my budget: the kind of plugin, one specific example, and why it would change my sound. If my setup already covers everything, tell me to skip buying.

   Rules:
   - Recommend one purchase at most. Never a list or a bundle.
   - Never recommend something just because it's on sale.
   - Don't guess sale prices. Tell me to check the price myself.
   - Flag anything you're not sure about: a plugin you don't know, or a feature you can't confirm.
   ```

   Both free plans take screenshots. ChatGPT's free plan allows 3 uploads a day, so if you have more screenshots than that, use Claude (up to 20 files per chat).

3. Check the one plugin it picks before you buy. Open the developer's page, make sure the price fits your budget, and listen to a demo.

## More prompts
Tempted by another deal? Ask in the same chat:

```prompt
I'm about to buy [PLUGIN]. My plugin list is above in this chat (if not, here it is: [PASTE YOUR PLUGIN LIST]). Which plugins I already own do the same job, and what would I actually gain? If I don't need it, say so.
```

## Watch out
- **AI can get plugins wrong.** It can mix up features or misread a name in your screenshots. Check the developer's page before you skip a deal or buy one.
- **A sale price isn't a reason to buy.** If the audit says you're covered, keep your money.
- **Grab the free one.** On Friday, Nov 27, Waves releases a brand-new plugin and gives away a limited number of licenses for free. Sign up now with **Waves free plugin sign-up** below to get a reminder. Signing up isn't enough: on the 27th, log in to your Waves account and claim it.
- **Your chats can train the AI.** Both free plans can use your chats to improve their models unless you turn that off in settings. A plugin list is fine to share. Leave out license keys.
